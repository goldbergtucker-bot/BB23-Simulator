# Big Brother 23 Custom Simulator — V16

V16 is the unified season-engine release. V15 remains the backup.

## Implemented gameplay

- Four-team opening: Jokers, Aces, Kings and Queens
- Team-captain competitions and captain draft sequence
- Team safety during Weeks 1–4
- Wildcard Competition before nominations during Weeks 1–4
- Premiere Double or Nothing with accept/win, accept/lose and decline outcomes
- High Roller's Room during Weeks 6–8
- Weekly America BB Bucks awards with banked balances
- Veto Derby: 50 BB Bucks, six-player betting qualification, featured bet and Second Veto
- Chopping Block Roulette: 125 BB Bucks, nominee removal and random roulette replacement
- Coin of Destiny: 250 BB Bucks, challenge to the reigning HOH, secret HOH takeover and re-nomination
- Coin dethroned HOH remains immune and is eligible for the following HOH
- Double Evictions in Weeks 9 and 10
- Nine-member jury: 11th through 3rd place
- Final HOH Parts 1–3 and final HOH's Final 2 decision
- Finale jury vote and $750,000 / $75,000 / $50,000 prize settings
- Portraits and event participants remain attached to each history record
- V16 save key is separate from V15 and older saves; older saves remain importable through the legacy fallback

## Important

The simulator still uses the existing custom-cast setup. If a saved season already contains four complete team rosters, V16 preserves those rosters. Otherwise it creates the four-team opening and captain draft automatically.
